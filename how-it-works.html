HOW IT WORKS

A straightforward path from hello to working together.

We use a clear, collaborative process to understand fit, set expectations and launch with a shared plan.

Connect

Tell us about your company, your experience and the kind of campaign opportunity you are exploring.

Confirm fit

We discuss campaign availability, audience, qualification criteria, geography, compliance expectations and operating requirements.

Agree on the plan

If there is a fit, both sides align on commercial terms, lead or transfer definitions, tracking and communication before launch.

Test and learn

Where appropriate, begin with an agreed test period and review performance against the campaign’s defined measures.

Develop the relationship

Continue, adjust or scale only when the results and operating relationship support the next step.

Campaign acceptance and outcomes are not guaranteed. Any launch depends on mutual agreement, campaign availability and applicable requirements.
